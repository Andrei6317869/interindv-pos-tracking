Beacon Network Simulator | multi-layer

Agents wander at random around a beacon. Two nodes are linked when they are within radio range. The matrix is the adjacency matrix of that graph, updated live. Added layers: connected components (subgraph detection), node-degree statistics, a leader chosen by connectivity, and a second graph whose nodes are the old groups. Simulated data only.

Subgraphs: connected components

    Layer 2: old groups (ST A S)

    Each participant belongs to one old group (A, B or C, shown next to the node). Group matrix W = STAS counts links between members of two groups; the diagonal is twice the links inside a group. Node size = total degree of the group's members, edge thickness = W.

Beacon Network Simulator | v3 groups and coordinator

People explore the area at random. A link is simply 1 (within range) or 0. A coordinator walks in the area but has no links and belongs to no group; she walks to a key location, places a temporary beacon there, and the beacon calls the people with the requested skills, locally or across the whole arena. The request can hop from person to person along links, a chosen number of hops. Each person may decline. When the call ends the group and its leader (most links to people outside the group) are fixed, the beacon disappears, the group works at the site, then it is released. Simulated data only.

Fill colour = skill. Dotted ring = asked, green ring = accepted, grey ring = declined; h1, h2... = number of hops the request travelled to reach that person (red lines = relays). Dark ring with LEADER = leader, fixed when the group is formed. A line = linked now (within range). The black square is the coordinator: she walks in the arena and calls people to a place, but has no links and is not part of the connectivity graph.

Layers (coordinator, groups, people)

Layer 0: the coordinator (in the space, but not a node of the connectivity graph). Layer 1: each active group collapsed into one node. Layer 2: the people.

Run statistics

Leader switches are counted while a group works, to show why the leader is fixed: a leader re-chosen every moment by current links would keep changing.
